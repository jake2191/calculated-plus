# Calculated+ Support

A calculator, notes page, dice roller and card drawer for magicians and mentalists.

## Getting started

Full instructions are inside the app. The first time you open it, it shows you where to find the performer settings. From there, **Your First Trick** walks you through a complete routine in about a minute.

## Common questions

**I've forgotten how to open the settings.** Delete and reinstall the app, and the first-launch hint appears again. You'll need to set your preferences up again afterwards.

**A widget isn't doing anything.** Open the app once after adding or changing a widget, and check the widget's page in the performer settings.

**Does the app collect my data?** No. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
