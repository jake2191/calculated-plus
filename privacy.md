# Calculated+ Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

Calculated+ does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Everything you set up in the app stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

## Data kept on your device

**Settings and history.** Your preferences, the text you type into the app (such as notes, widget content and to-do items) and your dice and card history are saved only on your device, so they're there next time you open the app.

**Photos.** If you add photos to the Photos widget, you choose them with Apple's photo picker. The app only sees the photos you pick. It stores a resized copy on your device so the widget can show it. It never reads the rest of your library.

**Motion sensors.** Some features read your phone's motion sensors. This is processed instantly on the device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

**Audio.** One optional feature plays silent audio while the app is open. The app never records audio and never uses the microphone.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
